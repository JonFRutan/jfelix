The Conception of DoodleBrawl

January 2026 - VCU is hosting it's annual 24-hour hackathon. A hackathon is a competition where teams create a software project in accordance with a given theme. We were provided multiple general themes: Social Impact, AI, and Sustainability. They had more specific themes under each of these categories but we also had free rein to choose our own topic.

None of us on the team (I'll talk more about them in a bit) were too agog with any of the project ideas, so we took a couple of hours to brainstorm something that fit within the themes and was more "our speed". This result would be DoodleBrawl, a wholly AI run fight-promotion.

The Team

The DoodleBrawl team.

Let's introduce the protagonists of the tale of DoodleBrawl:

  • Jon Rutan - Me. I worked on the server, backend, and AI APIs.
  • Connor Fair - A friend I had made through AI class. He worked on the designs, styling, and presentation of our project. He also contributed to the backend.
  • Trevor Corcoran - My partner-in-crime at VCU. We ran the Linux User Group together and attended dozens of events. He did the frontend work.
  • Gandalf - We left this video running throughout it's entire 10-hour duration. I would be remiss to not accredit him as a part of the time.

I had such a blast with these guys. I am still amazed at how talented they are, and how tirelessly they worked on such a silly project. I couldn't have had a better team and they made the 24-hours blow by.
