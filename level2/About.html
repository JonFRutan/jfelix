The Site

This site is an independent project of mine that started as an excercise in VM management, web hosting, and networking. It's become a bit of a creative outlet for me, and has the goal of being a more robust and accessible portfolio of my work.

This site's primary goal is ease of navigation and accessibility- ideally serving as a hypertext résumé.

The secondary goal of the site is to serve as a public facing entrance towards my personal web servers and machines. (Coming soon...)
